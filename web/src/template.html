    Rail size
    Sturdy rails are taller and wider, and the walls 0.4 mm thicker. Modules with different rails do not fit together.

    Modules only lock together if they share the unit size, depth and rail size. Clearances can differ between batches.

    Printing and assembly in four steps
    1. Print the test kit first and note which groove piece slides best on the rail. If it is not 0.25 mm, set it under 3 Print.
    2. Print with a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, 3 wall loops and no supports. Do not rotate or scale the files.
    3. Lay out the base. Build from the bottom up and from left to right, sliding each empty casing in from the front until it clicks. Fit the side panels as you go and slide the top panels on last.
    4. Put the drawers in last. A drawer stops about three-quarters open; a firm pull takes it out.
